# FAA Drone Documentation Index Source Plan

Research date: July 13, 2026

## Scope

- Current operational documentation for drones and unmanned aircraft systems (UAS) only.
- FAA-hosted sources only.
- Excludes Advanced Air Mobility, historical archives, superseded guidance, and external eCFR or Federal Register pages.
- FAA PDFs can be indexed directly by FsVoice. FAA HTML pages must first be downloaded, stripped of navigation and footer content, and converted to Markdown or structured JSON.

## Exclusions

Do not include the following in the current operational corpus:

- AC 91-57C, AC 107-2, Order 8130.34C, or earlier revisions.
- The 2016 Part 107 summary PDF as authoritative guidance.
- Old integration roadmaps, Aviation Rulemaking Committee reports, Integration Pilot Program reports, and expired notices.
- UAS sightings spreadsheets and quarterly activity statistics.
- Individual waivers, exemptions, environmental assessments, and type-certification notices.
- Interactive maps, B4UFLY applications, DroneZone, and application portals. Their descriptive FAA pages are indexable, but their live operational data is not.
- News releases, proposed rules, and proposed-rule fact sheets unless explicitly tagged `proposed` rather than current law.

## Freshness and Authority Rules

- Use the FAA [Policy Document Library](https://www.faa.gov/uas/resources/policy_library) for discovery only.
- Validate every circular, order, and notice against its FAA document landing page and include only records marked active.
- Prefer the newest active revision and record the document number, revision, issue date, status, source URL, retrieval timestamp, and content hash.
- Re-crawl HTML pages regularly and re-check active document status before rebuilding an index.
- Never allow a superseded document to share the same current-authority tier as its replacement.

The Policy Document Library still links AC 91-57C even though AC 91-57D replaced it, which is why blind recursive downloading is unsafe.
